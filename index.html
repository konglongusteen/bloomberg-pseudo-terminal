COMMAND DIRECTORY & FEATURE GUIDE

📌 BASIC CONTROLS

• Click any symbol in the watchlist to view its chart.
• Use BUY/SELL buttons or keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+B (Buy), Ctrl+S (Sell), Ctrl+A (Sell All).
• The order total updates automatically as you change quantity.
• Toggle between dark/light mode using the 🌓 button.
• Save your panel layout (resized columns, collapsed modules) with 💾 SAVE WS – it will restore on next login.

👤 PROFILE SETTINGS

Click the 👤 PROFILE button to open your profile modal. There you can:
• Change your username (you will be logged out and need to log in again).
• Change your password (requires current password, then re‑login).
• Two‑Factor Authentication (2FA): Enable TOTP via Google Authenticator for extra security.
• Permanently delete your account – all portfolio, trade history, and snapshots will be erased.

⏳ CONDITIONAL ORDERS

Set automatic exit orders for positions you already hold:
• Stop Loss: Sell when price falls below a trigger.
• Take Profit: Sell when price rises above a trigger.
• Trailing Stop (%): Sell if price drops X% from its highest price since order creation.
Orders are checked every 5 seconds and execute as market sells. Active orders are shown and can be cancelled.

💼 MY ASSETS

Displays all your current holdings with quantity, total value, and daily P&L. Click any asset to jump to its chart. Use the search box to filter by symbol. This module appears in the left panel (desktop) and is reordered in portrait mode between the trade module and strategy builder.

📈 CORRELATION MATRIX (30-day)

Shows how closely your top 5 holdings + S&P 500 move together.
• Green = positive correlation (move in same direction).
• Red = negative correlation (move opposite).
• Use it to detect over‑concentration: if many stocks are highly correlated (e.g., >0.7), your portfolio may lack diversification.
• Click the ⟳ button to refresh the matrix. The panel is collapsible.

📊 STRATEGY BUILDER (Backtester)

Simulate trading strategies using historical data (last 180 days). Choose a symbol, indicator, and quantity, then click RUN or GO.
• SMA Crossover: Buys when 20-day SMA crosses above 50-day SMA, sells on opposite cross.
• RSI: Buys when RSI drops below 30 (oversold), sells when RSI rises above 70 (overbought).
• Bollinger Bands: Buys when price touches lower band, sells when price touches upper band.
• Results show total P&L, total trades, win rate, average P&L per trade, max drawdown, and a full trade log.

🤖 AI COPILOT (Groq)

• Ask market questions – the AI will answer concisely.
• Use slash commands: /buy 5 AAPL or /sell 10 TSLA to execute trades instantly.
• Natural language like "buy 2 shares of NVDA" will also work and generate clickable buttons.
• Advanced actions: "change chart to 3M", "add BTC-USD to watchlist", "run SMA crossover backtest on AAPL with 5 shares".
• Rate‑limited to 3 queries per minute.

📉 TECHNICAL INDICATORS & FORECAST

• RSI (14), MACD, Bollinger Bands (20,2) are shown below the chart.
• Forecast models: Linear regression (frontend) or ARIMA (backend – uses Yahoo data). Toggle Forecast to see a 5‑day price projection on the chart.
• You can switch between candlestick and line chart.

📋 PORTFOLIO & TRADE HISTORY

• Your cash balance and holdings are displayed in the footer. Click any holding to jump to its chart.
• The TRADE HISTORY button opens a modal with all your past trades. Each trade has a Verify button – this checks the cryptographic signature (SHA-256 hash chain) to ensure the trade record has not been tampered with (audit trail).
• The Portfolio Value History chart shows your total portfolio value over time (1W/1M/3M ranges). The Portfolio Composition doughnut chart shows the percentage of cash vs each holding, with a colour legend on the right in landscape mode.

📊 PORTFOLIO RISK METRICS

Quantitative evaluation of your trading performance:
• Sharpe Ratio – risk-adjusted return (higher is better, >1 is good).
• Sortino Ratio – focuses only on downside volatility.
• Max Drawdown – largest peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value.
These are calculated from your daily portfolio snapshots. The risk‑free rate uses the 10‑year Treasury yield (FRED) or a default 2%.

🏦 MACRO DASHBOARD (FRED)

Live economic indicators from the Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED):
• 10Y & 2Y Treasury yields – key for bond markets and recession signals (inverted curve when 2Y > 10Y).
• CPI (YoY) – Consumer Price Index, a primary inflation measure.
• Fed Funds Rate – central bank policy rate.
Data updates daily, cached for 24h. Requires a free FRED API key (set FRED_API_KEY in .env).

📄 CORPORATE FUNDAMENTALS

Deep dive into any stock’s financial health using Alpha Vantage data:
• Enter a symbol (e.g., AAPL) and click Fetch.
• Displays Market Cap, P/E, EPS, Dividend Yield, Beta, 52‑week range, Revenue, Profit Margin, Debt/Equity.
• Data is cached for 24 hours to respect API rate limits. Requires a free Alpha Vantage key (ALPHA_VANTAGE_KEY in .env).

🔍 WATCHLIST & FILTERS

• 50 US stocks + 4 major indices.
• Use search, favourites (★), gainers/losers filter, and asset type filter (stock/index).
• Click the refresh (⟳) button to reload all quotes.
• Symbols flash green (up) or red (down) on price changes.

💾 PERSISTENT WORKSPACE

• Resize the left/center/right panels by dragging the gray handles. Collapse any expandable section (Strategy Builder, Correlation Matrix).
• Click 💾 SAVE WS to store your panel sizes and collapsed states to your user profile. On next login, your workspace will be restored exactly as you left it.

🏆 LEADERBOARD

Compares all registered users by their latest portfolio total value. Shows 24h absolute and percentage change. Sorted descending by total value. Access via the trophy button in the header.

📰 NEWS & TOP MOVERS

• Latest financial news from Finnhub (refreshes every 2 minutes).
• Top 5 gainers and losers from your watchlist are displayed in real time.
